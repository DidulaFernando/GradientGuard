7. INTERPRET THE REPORT
noise = RMS across six runwise feature_A-feature_B differences / sqrt(2).
spread = sample SD across six runwise panel-average features (ddof=1).
Raw ratios are reported; decisions use spread / max(noise, numerical floor).
Level numerical floor: 1e-5 share units. Slope floor: 2e-5 / observed fraction span.
A larger measured identical-probe feature jitter replaces that floor.
Zero/flat/nonfinite observations are explicitly flagged; no interpolation or cherry-picking.
At least one retained level AND one slope must exceed the provisional ratio 3 screen.

Correctness fails -> repair before training.
Identical-probe unstable -> resolve numerical/stochastic behavior.
Reliability improves with larger probes -> document/adopt revised measurement budget.
Sampling dominates at 256/class -> reconsider measurement feasibility.
Stable levels but unstable/flat slopes -> temporal feasibility unresolved.
Head/bias concern -> targeted control before interpreting layer redistribution.
Reliable levels and slopes surviving diagnostics -> larger completed-training outcome pilot.
Absent background contrast -> investigate the manipulation.
Excessive monitoring cost -> revise budget, then revalidate.

This is descriptive engineering evidence from TWO fixed settings and three paired seeds,
not six independent setting families. It does not validate final WGA prediction,
methodological novelty, improved audit discovery or a causal learning mechanism.
